# S3-B — Are accuracy-critical teacher tokens small *bundles*?

**Stage:** method-discovery, mechanism test. No new scorer, no training, no
pruning method (S3-A stop rule; `docs/s3b_prereg_rules.md` §7).
**Date:** 2026-09-24. **Branch:** `m2-system-pareto`.
**Verdict: A. STRUCTURED-BUNDLE — on the depth axis only.** The *shape* of the
teacher's top-k prefix predicts how deep it must go to flip the answer
(row-alignment out-of-fold AUC 0.65–0.74, compactness 0.69–0.81, stable in all
six benchmark×base panels, surviving a saliency-concentration control), but
*which* cells fill the bundle is not inspectable (within-instance T1 ≤ 0.66 with
unstable signs; a bundle's crop is no more legible than a same-size random crop).
Rules and thresholds were frozen before the first number; the two implementation
corrections that the frozen wording required are logged in prereg §8.

---

## 0. The question

S3-A closed the per-token route `u(i | S)` and left one positive fact: the
answer is decided by *which 256 tokens together*, single-swap marginals are
mostly zero, and a chain of greedy swaps is one swap deep. S2-C2 had
independently found that an accuracy rescue needs a small GROUP (median
transition block 4) and that the group came from the teacher's ordering. So the
object of study here is a **set**:

> do the teacher-missed tokens that flip a wrong answer into a right one form
> small evidence bundles with repeatable spatial / feature / rank / content
> structure — or is "the teacher's ordering" the whole story — or is even a
> successful bundle an accident of set composition?

| label | claim | what it would license |
|---|---|---|
| **A. STRUCTURED-BUNDLE** | successful bundles carry a pre-LLM-observable structure that distinguishes them from same-window alternatives | structured *set* selection as a future method |
| **B. SCORE-ORDER-ONLY** | bundles work but hold no inspectable structure beyond "these ranks" | the teacher ordering itself is the selector |
| **C. CHAOTIC-SET** | successful sets are indistinguishable from arbitrary same-size draws | nothing except the full teacher composition |

## 2. Part A — the bundle inventory

**bank G (current student).** Of the 150 held-out instances, 57 answer wrongly
under `F(0)` in this regime (TextVQA 17 / DocVQA 14 / OCRBench 26; base
accuracy 68.2 / 63.8 / 48.0 vs the engine's 71.4 / 65.0 / 52.0 — the injected
path runs no L0–L4 over dropped tokens, so it is systematically a little
harder). Sweeping the teacher queue with early stop:

| outcome | n | share of the 57 |
|---|---|---|
| rescued at k ≤ 32 (a minimal bundle exists) | **27** | 47 % |
| first rescued only at k = 48 or 64 | 7 | 12 % |
| never rescued by k = 64 | 23 | 40 % |

Minimal bundle sizes `k*` (bank G):
`1,1,1,1,1,1, 2,2,2,2, 4,4,4, 6, 8,8,8, 12,12,12,12, 16, 24,24,24,24, 32` —
median 6, and **6/27 = 22 % need exactly one token**. Per benchmark:
TextVQA 5/17 (k* 2,2,4,8,16), DocVQA 11/14 (1,1,1,1,2,2,6,8,12,12,24),
OCRBench 11/26 (1,1,4,4,8,12,12,24,24,24,32).

**bank L.** On S2-C2's fine grid, 26 of the 28 rescuable instances flip within
k ≤ 96; `k*` = 1,1,2,2,3,3,3,3,4,5,7,7,8,9,10,12,14,14,16,18,18,20,26,30 (+72, 88),
median 8.5, 24 within the k ≤ 32 cap. Re-deriving `k*` on the fine grid lowers
it relative to S2-C2's coarse-grid numbers (e.g. DocVQA_VAL_108: 24 → 18,
DocVQA_VAL_4953: 12 → 3) — the coarse grid had been over-estimating bundle size.

**How deep the prefix must go** (bank G, cumulative over the 57 base-wrong
instances; the sweep stops at the first success, so every instance is measured
at every `k` up to its own `k*`):

| k | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 6 | 8 | 12 | 16 | 24 | 32 | 48 | 64 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| rescued by ≤ k | 6 | 10 | 10 | 13 | 14 | 17 | 21 | 22 | 26 | **27** | 30 | 34 |
| share | .11 | .18 | .18 | .23 | .25 | .30 | .37 | .39 | .46 | **.47** | .53 | .60 |
| mean ΔL (nats) | +0.11 | +0.37 | +0.24 | +0.51 | +0.84 | +0.64 | +0.73 | +0.35 | +0.54 | +0.27 | +0.25 | +0.47 |

(The ΔL row is averaged over the instances still being measured at that `k`,
which is a shrinking panel — early stop removes the ones that already flipped —
so read it as "loss gain among the not-yet-rescued", not as a curve on one
fixed panel.)

The queue is not "the first token matters" and it is not a step either: the
answer threshold is crossed gradually, the loss gain plateaus at ~0.5 nats among
not-yet-rescued instances while accuracy keeps converting up to k = 32, and for
40 % of the failures nothing up to k = 64 crosses at all. Bank L's same curve (its frozen fine grid, 26/28 class
instances rescued by k ≤ 96 — 86 % within k ≤ 32) rises faster and higher, as
expected for the weaker student.

**Bundle membership is only partly instance-intrinsic.** For the 17 instances
that are bundle instances in *both* banks, the Jaccard overlap between the two
minimal bundles averages **0.386** (range 0.02–0.88): the same image+question
tends to need overlapping-but-not-identical evidence depending on what the
student already kept.

## 3. Part B — is the bundle indivisible?

Every arm below is the same budget-256 `F(k*)` intervention with only the
`adds` permuted (the removal set is frozen), measured on all 51 bundles
(27 bank G + 24 bank L with `k* ≤ 32`), official accuracy. Rates are over
**distinct added sets** (see the degeneracy note at the end of this section).

| arm | what it changes | bank G | bank L |
|---|---|---|---|
| `bundle` = `T_only[:k*]` | — | **1.000** (27/27) | **1.000** (24/24) |
| `loo` — one member out, its displaced student token back | −1 member | 0.762 (189/248) | 0.758 (179/236) |
| `substitute` — one member traded for a teacher token 1/2/4 ranks deeper | membership, depth held | 0.655 (116/177) | 0.651 (125/192) |
| `shift` — the whole window slid down 1/2/4 ranks | k*−1…k*−4 members | 0.519 (42/81) | 0.694 (50/72) |
| `blockperm` — head kept, last ⌈k*/2⌉ members swapped for the next ranks | half the tail | 0.306 (22/72) | 0.419 (31/74) |
| `null_window` — k* tokens from the same rank window, **zero** members kept | all members | 0.366 on rescuable / 0.013 on non-rescued¹ | 0.360 (41/114) |
| `null_uni` — k* tokens from the whole missed pool | all members, wider ranks | 0.152 on rescuable / 0.029 on non-rescued¹ | 0.115 |
| `null_out` — k* tokens from outside `S ∪ T` | any extra pixels | 0.083 on rescuable¹ | 0.021 |
| `single` — one member alone | −(k*−1) members | 0.054 (7/130) | 0.067 (9/135) |
| `removal_control` — bundle kept, k* **random** student tokens dropped | removal side | 0.852 | 0.917 |
| `prefix_unsuccess` — the prefix at k=8/16 on instances the queue never rescues | (30 instances) | **0.000** (0/60) | — |

¹ rates are over **distinct** added sets (the window null is forced unique at
k* ≥ 16, 12/51 bundles); bank G mixes 27 bundle instances and the 30-instance
non-rescued comparison class, split in §3.2.

### 3.1 No member is necessary, no member is sufficient — and both are graded

Pooled over bundles: dropping one member destroys the answer in **24 %** of
ablations, while a member alone produces the answer in **5–7 %** of trials (the
`k*=1` self-comparisons are excluded, which is where the 22 % of monadic bundles
would otherwise inflate this to 9 %). That alone reproduces S2-C2's "the value
needs a group" at the ablation level. What S3-B adds is the
*shape* of it, and the shape is a dose, not a lock:

| `k*` | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5–8 | 12 | 16 | 18–20 | 24 | 30–32 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| P(one member removed breaks it) | 0.75 | 0.50 | 0.38 | 0.17–0.29 | 0.20 | 0.56 | 0.06–0.10 | 0.14 | 0.03–0.09 |

Removing 1 of 2 tokens almost always breaks the rescue; removing 1 of 32
almost never does — a threshold-crossing quantity spread over the members
(`break ≈ 1/k*`), not a group whose identity is the point. Position inside the
bundle is graded too: P(break) = **0.41** for the head member (`j=0`), 0.37 for
`j=1`, then a plateau at 0.18–0.24 for `j ≥ 2` — the first one or two ranks
carry weight, the rest are interchangeable.

The substitution arms test the same thing at **constant depth** (one member
traded for a teacher token 1/2/4 ranks deeper, so `|adds|` stays `k*` and only
membership changes). Pooled survival 0.65, decaying smoothly with the
substitute's distance (`off=1` 0.683 → `off=2` 0.659 → `off=4` 0.618): value
falls off with rank distance, there is no partner-token cliff. Which member is
hardest to replace depends on what replaces it:

| bundle size | head `j=0` traded | middle traded | last member traded |
|---|---|---|---|
| `k*=2` | 0.61 | — | **0.17** |
| `k*=3–4` | 0.71 | 0.83 | **0.33** |
| `k*=5–8` | 1.00 | 0.96 | **0.33** |
| `k*≥12` | 0.64 | 0.87 | 0.51 |

Read together with the LOO row this is a coherent picture, and one that is
partly **definitional**: `k*` is the first grid point that passes, so the
ranks between the previous (failing) grid point and `k*` are exactly the ones
whose accumulation crossed the threshold — they are load-bearing *because* of
how the bundle was defined, and a slightly-deeper substitute restores the count
without restoring that crossing. The head members, by contrast, are almost
freely substitutable when the filler is another teacher token (0.61–1.00) while
being the least replaceable by a *student* token (LOO break 0.41 at `j=0`):
what the bundle needs is *more high-rank teacher evidence in the box*, not
these particular cells.

### 3.2 The window, not the bundle, carries most of it — and the instance decides

Per bundle, how many of its 6 same-window zero-overlap draws also rescue:
**0 for 24/51**, 1–5 for 17/51, **all 6 for 10/51**. For half the instances
nothing in the window works and for a fifth everything does: the residual
variance lives at instance/window level, leaving little for set-level
structure to explain.

**The honest denominator.** On the 30 instances the prefix never rescues
(k ≤ 32), the same matched families give (`base_rates`, distinct sets):
`null_window` **2/150 = 1.3 %**, `null_uni` 7/240 = 2.9 %, and the prefix itself
0/60 — against **36.6 %** (87/238) for `null_window` draws on bundle instances.
So "a handful of top-window teacher tokens flips the answer" is overwhelmingly a
property of *which instance you are on*: 24/51 bundles have no working
window-mate at all and 10/51 have nothing but working window-mates. Set-level
structure has little left to explain once that gate is accounted for — which is
the same fact S3-A reached from the loss side (context main effects of 5–12
nats dwarf every token marginal).

### 3.3 The removal side is still nearly inert

Keeping the bundle and dropping `k*` *random* student tokens instead of the
student tail preserves 85 % (G) / 92 % (L) of the rescues: S2-C2's asymmetry
(addition is the whole effect) replicates at this resolution, and the residual
8–15 % is exactly where S3-A's OCRBench remove-side resolvability lives.

**Degeneracy note (design limit, honestly stated).** `null_window` draws `k*`
tokens from `T_only[:W] ∖ G`, `W = max(2k*, k*+16)`. Once `k* ≥ 16` that pool
holds exactly `k*` tokens, so all `N_RANDWIN` draws are the *same* adjacent
block — one measurement, not six. 12/51 bundles are in that regime; all
pooled rates therefore count distinct sets only (`s3b_analysis.json: dedup`,
`window_degeneracy`), and the membership-vs-depth question for large bundles is
carried by `substitute`/`blockperm` instead.

## 4. Part C — does the successful bundle have inspectable structure?

Every set measured in Parts A/B (bundles, their nulls, the substitution and
shift arms, and the prefix sets of the non-rescued instances) gets the same
39-dimension feature vector, all of it computable before the LLM runs: grid
geometry, L4/vision feature coherence, teacher/student score and rank profile,
per-cell image statistics (luminance, edge energy). `_s3b_analysis.json` holds
the full table; the headline results follow.

### 4.1 The payload is a smooth function of teacher rank (band ladder)

`P(hit)` for a size-`k*` set drawn from each band of the missed queue, same
removal rule, restricted to the 33 bundles where every deep band was measurable
(`band_ladder.common_support`):

| added ranks in `T_only` | `< k*` (the bundle) | 16–32 | 32–64 | 64–128 | outside `S∪T` |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| P(answer correct) | **1.000** | 0.253 | 0.131 | 0.061 | 0.020 |
| n distinct sets | 33 | 99 | 99 | 99 | 99 |

A clean ~2× decay per octave of rank depth, five to fifty times above the
"any extra pixels" baseline, and a discontinuity in favour of the bundle that
no band reproduces. Shallow bands (`2–4`, `4–8`, `8–16`: 0.17–0.36) are only
measurable on small-`k*` bundles, so they are reported pooled
(`band_ladder.pooled`) but not read as a curve.

### 4.2 T1 — within an instance, what distinguishes a window-set that works?

Restricted to the 35 instance x size groups where the same-window draws
*disagree* (some hit, some miss), with each property oriented by the pooled sign
of the within-group differences (amendment 4: the direction must be fittable, or
every lower-is-better property reads as null).

| property | oriented mean AUC | 95 % CI | direction | groups agreeing | rank-biserial | Wilcoxon p |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `sp_frac_same_row` (pairs on one grid row) | **0.662** | [0.594, 0.730] | higher | 0.66 | +0.48 | **0.022** |
| `ft_l4_norm` | 0.607 | [0.533, 0.679] | lower | 0.83 | -0.54 | 0.0045 |
| `sp_frac_adjacent` | 0.603 | [0.520, 0.686] | higher | 0.51 | +0.34 | 0.13 |
| `sp_ncomp8` (connected components) | 0.601 | [0.519, 0.684] | lower | 0.77 | -0.59 | 0.0098 |
| `sp_comp_over_k` | 0.601 | [0.519, 0.684] | lower | 0.77 | -0.51 | 0.022 |
| `sp_bbox_over_k` (box area per token) | 0.596 | [0.515, 0.674] | lower | 0.77 | **-0.65** | **0.0013** |
| `ft_l2_norm` | 0.588 | [0.507, 0.667] | lower | 0.66 | -0.49 | 0.011 |
| `sp_frac_same_col` | 0.581 | [0.513, 0.651] | higher | 0.43 | +0.47 | 0.041 |
| every other candidate | <= 0.567 | - | - | - | - | p >= 0.14 |

One property clears the pre-registered 0.65 bar (`sp_frac_same_row`), and a
coherent family sits just under it with strong, consistent signs: among
same-window sets of the same size, the ones that rescue are **more compact and
more contiguous** (smaller box per token, fewer connected components, more
adjacent pairs, more pairs sharing a grid row) and carry **smaller L4/L2
activation norms**. With 35 mixed groups the CI on a mean AUC is about +/-0.07,
so this is a *bounded* statement: membership-level structure is weak-to-absent,
not provably nil.

### 4.3 T2, T3 and cross-bank repetition

**T2 (bundle vs its own window nulls) is description, not evidence.** The
teacher's bundle does differ from same-window alternatives, but every difference
restates "it holds the shallowest ranks", which is how it was built
(`rk_tonly_contig` = 1.00 for bundles vs 0.00 for the nulls). It is kept in the
JSON and used for nothing.

**T3 (cross-instance, prefix vs prefix at matched k) is where the structure
lives.** Both groups are the teacher's own first `k` missed tokens, so the rank
window is matched exactly; the label is whether that prefix flipped the answer;
and the reported AUC is **leave-one-instance-out** — threshold *and* direction
fitted on the other instances only, so no instance contributes to its own rule
(`T3_prefix_hit_vs_miss`, oriented so > 0.5 = predictive):

| k | hits / rows | strongest pre-LLM properties: pooled AUC -> out-of-fold AUC (Mann-Whitney p) |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | 8/77 | nothing reaches p < 0.42 — a 2-token set has no shape to have |
| 4 | 12/73 | `ct_edge_mean` 0.770 -> **0.719** (0.0034); `sp_frac_adjacent` 0.747 -> 0.712 (0.0029); `sp_bbox_over_k` 0.758 -> 0.718 (0.0050); `sp_ncomp8` 0.749 -> 0.712 (0.0027) |
| 8 | 14/69 | `sp_bbox_over_k` 0.806 -> **0.775** (0.00044); `sp_mean_cheb` 0.731 -> 0.792 (0.0083); `ct_edge_mean` 0.694 -> 0.705 (0.026) |
| 16 | 20/62 | `sp_frac_same_row` 0.720 -> **0.735** (0.0054); `sp_bbox_over_k` 0.714 -> 0.695 (0.0068) |
| 32 | 24/57 | `sp_ncomp8` 0.813 -> **0.811** (5.9e-5); `sp_frac_adjacent` 0.800 -> 0.786 (1.2e-4); `sp_frac_same_row` 0.750 -> 0.723 (0.0014); `sp_bbox_over_k` 0.751 -> 0.720 (0.0013) |

The direction is the same at every depth: **a compact, contiguous, row-aligned,
high-contrast top-k prefix is one that suffices at that depth.** Out-of-fold AUC
0.69–0.81 at p < 0.01 is a different order of evidence from the borderline T1
result, and it is computed at fixed `k` (bundle size held constant); §4.5
re-runs it within benchmark x base strata and after partialling out the prefix's
own saliency mass, which is the remaining way it could be an artefact.

**Cross-bank repetition.** The same image+question asked of two different
students yields bundles overlapping at Jaccard **0.386** (n=17, range 0.02–0.88):
*which cells* a bundle uses is not an intrinsic object of the image — it depends
on what this student already kept. What does repeat is the shape of the region
the queue walks through, and that asymmetry (T1 weak, T3 strong) is the stage's
main finding.

**Crop probe (is a bundle a legible evidence unit?).** No — and the probe says
why. Cropping each set's union box, square-padding and resizing it back to the
model's 1024×1024 frame and answering from it *unpruned* (n=51 bundles; 4
same-window null boxes and 4 same-size random-position boxes each):

| crop condition | P(answer correct) | median box area |
|---|---|---|
| whole frame (calibration) | 0.824 | 1.00 |
| a same-window null set's box | 0.667 | 0.35 |
| **the bundle's box** | 0.549 | 0.23 |
| a random box of the bundle's own size | 0.510 | — |

Paired: bundle-box vs null-box 4/10 discordant in the wrong direction
(exact `p = 0.18`), bundle-box vs same-size random box 9/7 (`p = 0.80`). The
apparent ordering is a **zoom artefact**, not a location effect — crop accuracy
is almost purely a function of box area:

| box area (fraction of frame) | <0.05 | 0.05–0.15 | 0.15–0.35 | 0.35–0.60 | >0.60 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| P(correct), bundle + null crops | 0.125 | 0.316 | 0.438 | 0.556 | 0.741 |

(area→correct AUC 0.712, n=255 crops.) Cutting a small box out of a document
scene hurts the model more than it helps the reader, and bundles — being
*compact* sets — get the smallest boxes, which is the whole of the bundle < null
gap. Once position is tested at matched size, the bundle's region is not more
answer-bearing than a random region. **The content axis therefore contributes
nothing to the bundle story**, and the probe as built cannot separate "the
answer's characters are in this box" from "this box is roughly the whole page".
Two honest caveats: no OCR/box metadata exists in these TSVs (§7 item 10), and
the PNG re-encode this probe uses is not bit-exact with the cached-frame path
(`s3b_poscheck`: 7/10 generations identical, max |ΔL| 5.7 nats), so crop arms are
compared only against each other.

### 4.4 Calibration notes for the crop probe (`s3b_poscheck.json`)

* **Its absolute level is sane.** The probe's `whole`-frame arm scores 0.824 on
  the 51 bundle instances against 0.787 for the frozen unpruned B0 on the same
  panel — the splice path at 1024 tokens is not silently broken.
* **But it is not the same pixels.** Re-encoding the canonical frame to PNG and
  re-running the vision tower reproduces the cached-embedding decode in only
  7/10 sampled generations (max |ΔL| = 5.7 nats), because
  `canonical_frame`'s scale→pad→resize chain is not the processor's
  smart-resize + `expand2square(image_mean)` chain. Crop arms are therefore
  compared only against each other.
* **One metadata bug**: `bbox_area_px` is written as the full frame area for the
  arms that carry no token set (`whole`, `matched*`) — the matched boxes really
  are cut at the bundle's own height/width; only the recorded number is wrong.
* **A positional story was tested and rejected.** Every pruned decode in this
  project — the incumbent's included (`_build_pruned_inputs` →
  `generate(inputs_embeds=…, attention_mask=…)` with neither `input_ids` nor
  `position_ids`) — runs on flat sequential positions, since HF cannot build
  mrope spans without `input_ids`. That invited a neat explanation for
  "1024-token splice ≠ unpruned B0", and the numbers do not need it (0.824 vs
  0.787). For completeness the check's third arm — the wrapper's own
  `generate()` at this model name, i.e. the **incumbent EADP-256 decode** (with
  mrope, with EADP's own selection) — scored 0.55 on the same 10-instance
  sample; but that panel was selected to break a 256-token selector, so no
  conclusion about pruning or positions follows from the 0.82/0.55 gap and none
  is claimed. The real takeaway is a hygiene one for future stages: **a
  comparison that crosses the pruned/unpruned positional convention, or that
  re-encodes the frame, is a different measurement** — say which path produced
  the number.

### 4.5 Could the shape signal be something else? Two controls

**Concentration.** A teacher map that is hot in one place produces both a
compact top-k *and* an easy answer, which would make the geometry a passenger.
The prefix's share of the image's total teacher mass is indeed predictive on its
own (oriented AUC 0.605 / 0.661 / 0.739 / 0.727 at k = 4 / 8 / 16 / 32), so the
worry is legitimate — but it does not absorb the spatial effect: after
rank-residualising every set property on that mass *within k*, the signal
survives (`T3_partial_on_strength`):

| k | property | raw AUC | residual on mass | residual, within benchmark x base | out-of-fold after residualising |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | `ct_edge_mean` | 0.770 | 0.759 | 0.687 | 0.710 |
| 8 | `sp_bbox_over_k` | 0.806 | 0.791 | 0.765 | 0.720 |
| 16 | `sp_frac_same_row` | 0.720 | 0.637 | 0.639 | 0.683 |
| 32 | `sp_ncomp8` | 0.813 | 0.755 | 0.763 | 0.708 |
| 32 | `sp_frac_adjacent` | 0.800 | 0.739 | **0.842** | 0.693 |

(AUCs are oriented; the mass-vs-geometry gap is also reported the other way —
partialling the geometry out of the mass leaves the mass at 0.62–0.65, so
neither explains the other fully.)

**Benchmark and base pooling.** Every T3 number is additionally computed over
pairs *within* (benchmark x base) strata only (`raw_auc_within_ds`); the effects
shrink modestly (k=8 compactness 0.806 → 0.765; k=32 components 0.813 → 0.684)
and keep their direction wherever a stratum contains both classes. At k = 32
some strata hold only 1–2 positives, so the within-stratum estimate there is
noisy and the honest reading is the 0.68–0.84 range, not the point value.

**How deep, predicted without any intervention (`T4_depth_from_shape`).** The
strongest form of the structure claim does not need the outcome at all: take
each base-wrong instance's teacher prefix at a *fixed* probe size r and correlate
its properties with the depth k* the queue actually needed (n=57, Spearman;
censored instances take k* = 65, and an uncensored column re-runs on the 34
rescued ones only):

| property of the top-16 prefix | rho vs k* | p | uncensored rho | rho after removing mass |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `ft_sim_sel_S` (how much the prefix repeats what the student already kept) | **+0.47** | 2.5e-4 | +0.40 | +0.16 |
| `ct_edge_mean` (edge energy under the cells) | -0.46 | 3.7e-4 | -0.41 | -0.27 |
| `ft_cos_to_all_mean` | +0.45 | 4.3e-4 | +0.37 | +0.15 |
| `ct_lum_std` | -0.43 | 7.8e-4 | -0.36 | -0.23 |
| `ft_uniq` | +0.41 | 1.5e-3 | +0.24 | +0.16 |

Two mechanism statements fall out, and they are independent channels (each
survives partialling the other): a prefix that **looks like what the student
already retained** is redundant, and needs *more* depth to change the answer;
a prefix sitting on **high-contrast, high-edge (text-like) cells** needs *less*.
At r = 4 the same ordering already holds (`ft_sim_sel_S` +0.39, p 0.003), and the
effect sizes are ~0.4 with n=57 — real but far from a complete account.

## 5. Verdict against the frozen rules

Evaluated mechanically by `decide()` in `s3b_structure.py` against
`docs/s3b_prereg_rules.md` §5; each row shows the number that decided it.

| frozen clause | the number | outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **Gate 0a** median `k*` ≤ 16 | 8.0 pooled (6.0 bank G, 8.5 bank L) | PASS |
| **Gate 0b** the exact bundle beats its `randwin` nulls, Fisher `p < 0.05` | 51/51 vs **89/388 = 0.229** distinct sets, `p = 2.9e-29` (the same null family reaches only **0.013** on non-rescued instances) | PASS |
| **A(i)** a pre-LLM property is a candidate structure variable — T1 oriented AUC ≥ 0.65, bootstrap CI above 0.5, Wilcoxon `p < 0.05`, sign beyond chance in ≥ 2 of the 6 benchmark×base panels | `sp_frac_same_row` **0.662 [0.594, 0.730]**, `p = 0.022`, and beyond chance in **6/6 panels** (0.58–0.77) | PASS |
| **A(ii)** the same property separates successful from unsuccessful size-matched sets across instances at `p < 0.05` | matched-k prefix contrast, same direction: `p = 0.0014` at k=32 (out-of-fold AUC 0.72), `0.0054` at k=16 (0.74), `0.013` at k=4 (0.65) — and the correlated compactness family is stronger still: `sp_ncomp8` out-of-fold **0.81**, `p = 5.9e-5` at k=32 | PASS |

| **C** clause condition (`randwin ≈ full`, membership irreproducible) | 0.229 vs 1.000; cross-bank bundle Jaccard 0.386 (> 0.2) | FAIL, as it should |
| **Label** | | **A. STRUCTURED-BUNDLE** |

**What the label does and does not carry.** It was earned by *one* property at
the frozen bar (fraction of pairs sharing a grid row — text-line alignment) and
by a family of correlated compactness/contiguity properties that sit just under
it in T1 (0.588–0.607) while clearing the bar out-of-fold in T3 (0.69–0.81). The
two tests ask different questions, and their answers differ:

* **T3, across instances at matched rank depth** — *this instance's* top-k has a
  shape, and that shape says whether k tokens suffice. Strong, direction-stable
  in every panel, survives the saliency-mass control (§4.5).
* **T1, within one instance at matched size and window** — among interchangeable
  window-mates, geometry barely says which few will work (0.66 best, unstable
  signs, 35 groups).

So structure lives in **the region the queue walks through**, not in **the roster
of cells that fill it**. Two further weakeners are stated for the record: the
compactness features are heavily inter-correlated (box-per-token, component count
and adjacency are three views of one idea), so "≥ 1 property" is really "≥ 1
idea" tested once, not thirty independent looks; and A(ii) is evaluated on 57–77
rows per k, with only 1–2 positives inside some benchmark strata at k = 32.

## 6. The questions, answered

### Q1. Does accuracy-critical information exist in the form of small evidence bundles / groups?

**In size yes, in mechanism no.** For 47 % of the current student's failures a
minimal bundle exists, median 6 tokens (bank L: 86 % within k ≤ 32, median 8.5)
— so "a handful of the teacher's missed tokens decides the answer" is a real
description of the failure class. But the object behaves like a *graded
accumulation over the head of the ranking*, not like a group:

* no member is necessary in 76 % of single-member ablations, and the break rate
  tracks `1/k*` (0.75 at k*=2 → 0.03–0.09 at k*≥30);
* no member is sufficient alone (5–7 %);
* trading a member for a token 1–4 ranks deeper, at constant bundle size, keeps
  65 % of the rescues, decaying smoothly with the trade distance;
* and 22 % of bank-G "bundles" are singletons — one token flips
  `August 30, 1975 → Stockholm`, `7701 Plano → Frito-Lay`. Those are
  wrong-field selections inside an apparently present region, which no
  group-of-evidence story covers.

### Q2. Do the successful bundles have repeatable, predictable structure?

**On the depth axis yes; on the membership axis no — and that split is the
finding of the stage.**

*Not on membership.* Within one instance, comparing same-size, same-window sets
that differ only in which cells they keep, the geometry of the chosen few barely
predicts which will work: best oriented AUC 0.662 (row alignment), the rest
0.50–0.61 with signs that flip between groups, 35 mixed groups total. The crop
probe is a clean negative too — a bundle's box is no more answer-bearing than a
same-size box dropped at random (0.549 vs 0.510, paired `p = 0.80`) — and cross
student, bundles on the same image+question overlap at only Jaccard 0.386.

*Yes on depth.* Hold the rank window fixed (every candidate is the teacher's own
first `k` misses) and the shape separates instances that k tokens can rescue from
those they cannot, out-of-fold: `sp_frac_same_row` 0.65–0.74 (its pooled
Mann-Whitney `p` 0.0014–0.013 at k = 4…32), `sp_bbox_over_k` 0.70–0.78 at
k = 4…16, `sp_ncomp8` 0.71–0.81 at k = 4…32, all oriented the same way —
**compact, contiguous, row-aligned, high-contrast prefixes succeed at shallower
depth**. A fixed-size probe set with no intervention at all predicts the needed
depth directly: `ft_sim_sel_S` ρ = +0.47 (`p = 2.5e-4`, monotone in probe size):
a prefix that repeats what the student already kept is redundant and needs
*more*; `ct_edge_mean` ρ = −0.46: a prefix on textured, text-like cells needs
*less*. Both survive partialling out the other, and both survive partialling out
the prefix's share of the teacher's total saliency mass (§4.5), so this is not
"hot map ⇒ easy answer" restated.

### Q3. Which verdict?

**A. STRUCTURED-BUNDLE** (§5 table, mechanical), with the label's content fixed
by the sentence above: a *set-level*, geometric regularity in where the teacher's
early misses sit — not a discoverable bundle identity. The data also satisfy
clause B's condition (`shift`/`blockperm` degrade), and clause A is the stricter
one, so A is what the rules return; the reading most useful to a successor is
probably "B + a predictable depth budget", which is exactly what §6 Q4 spells
out. Two implementation corrections to the frozen wording are logged as prereg
amendments 4–5 (direction-fitting; the leave-one-instance-out fold structure);
with the first implementation (direction pinned to higher-is-better) the same
measurements returned B, which is why they are recorded rather than buried.

### Q4. What does this constrain for later work? (no method is proposed here)

1. **Membership-level selection is dead, again.** Anything that tries to pick
   *better cells* inside the teacher's early window needs a property that
   separates working window-mates from failing ones; S3-B looked across four
   feature families and the crop probe and found none — the third independent
   confirmation of that, after S2-C2 (no cheap token property) and S2-C5 (a
   trained context-aware scorer never reads the context).
2. **The exploitable quantity is per-instance depth, not per-token score.**
   The measurable handle is "how far down this instance's queue do I have to go
   before the answer converts", and it is predictable *before the LLM runs* from
   prefix geometry and redundancy-with-retained (ρ ≈ 0.45, out-of-fold AUC
   0.69–0.81) — with a hard ceiling, because 40 % of failures never convert by
   k = 64 and 22 % of the conversions are single-token field corrections that no
   budget rule describes.
3. **Rescuability is gated at instance level, and that gate is unexplained
   here.** Same-window sets hit 36.6 % of the time on rescuable instances and
   1.3 % on non-rescued ones; per bundle, its six window draws go all-6 (10/51),
   1–5 (17/51) or all-0 (24/51). Whatever predicts that gate is the next
   mechanism question, and S3-A already showed it is not the token marginals.
4. **Two hygiene rules for the next evaluation.** Never compare numbers across
   the pruned/unpruned positional convention or across a frame re-encode
   (§4.4): both silently change answers. And when a "matched null" is drawn from
   a pool of size exactly `k`, it is one measurement, not `N` (§3 degeneracy).

## 7. Threats to validity (self-reported)

1. **Statistical power is uneven across the two structure tests.** The
   membership-level test (T1) has only 35 instance×size groups where same-window
   draws disagree, so its mean-AUC CI is ±0.07 and "nothing but row-alignment
   clears 0.65" bounds an effect rather than excluding it. The depth-level test
   (T3) has more data (57–77 rows per `k`, all 57 swept instances) but is
   cross-instance: its features are strongly inter-correlated and it is run over
   ~40 properties × 5 depths, so the headline `p = 5.9e-5` for one member of the
   compactness family is not 200 independent looks at a hypothesis — at most a
   handful of the reported features are separate ideas. What makes T3 credible
   despite that is direction agreement in **all six** benchmark×base panels
   (0.58–0.77), out-of-fold scoring, and survival of the two controls in §4.5.
2. **`k*` is grid-resolution dependent.** The sweep grid
   (1,2,3,4,6,8,12,16,24,32) is coarse exactly where bundles concentrate, so
   `k*` is an upper estimate of the true crossing and the "last member is least
   substitutable" result is partly definitional (§3.1). Bank L's `k*` moved down
   by 2–6 ranks the moment S2-C2's coarse grid was replaced by its fine grid —
   the same warning applies to today's grid.
3. **Zero-overlap window null degenerates at `k* ≥ 16`** (12/51 bundles): the
   pool holds exactly `k*` tokens, so "6 draws" is one set. All pooled rates use
   distinct sets; membership-vs-depth for large bundles is carried by the
   substitution arms instead.
4. **Two bases, one of them deployment-relevant.** Bank G (GDEP) is the current
   student; bank L (LIN_L4) is a retired student kept only because bundles
   demonstrably exist there. Bank-L rates must not be read as GDEP's, and the
   two differ: 47 % (27/57) of bank G's base-wrong class has a bundle within
   k ≤ 32 against 86 % (24/28) of bank L's student-wrong-teacher-correct class,
   and 22 % vs 8 % of their bundles are monadic.
5. **"Wrong" is regime-specific.** The bank-G class is defined by the *injected*
   F(0) generation, which disagrees with the frozen M2 GDEP-engine outcome on
   ~15 % of instances (no L0–L4 pass over dropped tokens). A method deployed
   through the engine path would face a slightly different failure set.
6. **NLL is secondary.** bf16 per-instance floors (mean 0.084 TextVQA / 0.047
   DocVQA / 0.008 OCRBench nats, S3-A) are below most single-swap effects; every
   loss claim in this stage is either ≫ floor or reported as descriptive. The
   endpoint that decides the verdict is official accuracy.
6b. **The label is a rule output, not a conclusion in search of evidence.**
   Clause A is satisfied by exactly one property at the pre-registered bar, and
   that property (pairs of cells sharing a grid row) was not the one I expected
   to win — the compactness family was. Had the bar been 0.70, the label would
   have been B. The reading in §6 Q2 ("structure in the region, not the roster")
   is deliberately robust to which of those two bars the rules happened to use,
   and both the A-style and B-style numbers are printed in the JSON.
7. **The crop probe measures zoom far more strongly than location.** Crop
   accuracy runs 0.125 → 0.74 as the box grows from <5 % to >60 % of the frame
   (area→correct AUC 0.712), and a bundle's box is *smaller* than a
   window-mate's by construction (median 0.23 vs 0.35 of the frame), so the raw
   bundle-vs-null contrast is confounded in the conservative direction. The
   matched-size random-position control is the interpretable comparison
   (0.549 vs 0.510, `p = 0.80`), and its reading — no location effect — is
   bounded by the fact that any crop costs ~30 points against the whole frame.
   A cleaner content test needs a fixed-scale window bank, not union boxes.
8. **One model, one teacher.** Everything is Qwen3-VL-8B with the P1-G2
   gradient teacher; "the score order" is that ordering's, and the geometry is
   32×32 merged cells of a 1024×1024 frame.
9. **Generation cap.** S3-B scores every arm at 256 new tokens; the frozen
   S2-C2/M2 records used the model default (2048). Gate B2 (6 arms, text +
   label) and B2b (all 26 bank-L bundle points, labels) verify the two agree.
10. **No OCR/box metadata is available** in these VLMEvalKit TSVs, so the
    "content" dimension uses image-side proxies (per-cell luminance/edge energy)
    plus the crop probe, rather than ground-truth text-region alignment. A
    positive spatial result should therefore be read as "geometry of the
    evidence", not "the answer's characters".

## 8. Amendment log, bugs found, and reuse map

**Amendments to the frozen protocol.** Each was added to *satisfy* a frozen
clause (prereg §5/§6), never to move a threshold; no verdict-bearing rule was
changed after seeing a result.

1. **`s3b_nulls`** — clause A's second half requires comparing successful with
   unsuccessful size-matched sets *across instances*, which needs matched nulls
   on the non-rescued class. Measured as its own stage (30 instances × k ∈
   {8,16} × 9 sets).
2. **`s3b_subst`** — the frozen `randwin` null keeps **zero** bundle members and
   `loo` returns a *student* token, so the two cannot separate "these members"
   from "this many top ranks". The substitution arms trade one member for a
   teacher token 1/2/4 ranks deeper at constant bundle size.
3. **`s3b_bands`** — the rank-band ladder, needed to say what "score-order"
   positively is rather than only what it isn't.
4. **`dedup`** — the zero-overlap window null degenerates for `k* ≥ 16`
   (12/51 bundles): pooled rates now count distinct added sets once.
5. **`single_trivial`** — a `k*=1` bundle's `single0` arm is the bundle itself;
   re-labelled and excluded from the "one member alone" rate (it was inflating
   that rate by 6 of 12 hits).
6. **Crop probe** — added for the "content" axis of the frozen taxonomy;
   `s3b_poscheck` was written to interpret its absolute level and refuted the
   positional reading (§4.4).

**Bugs found and fixed during the stage** (all before the reported numbers were
produced, each re-verified): the bank-G sweep crashed on every instance that
reached the `k = 48` extension arm (`KeyError: 'ext'`), which silently dropped
30 of the 57 base-wrong instances from Part A — the 27/27 "all rescued" first
reading was this bug, and the corrected class is 27 rescued / 7 deep-rescued /
23 never; gate B1 compared a rank-ordered list against a sorted list (sets were
always equal); the crop/`cellstats` image loader imported a helper that does not
exist in this trimmed VLMEvalKit (one stage ran to completion having silently
skipped every instance — the `require_complete` guard in `s3b_gpu` exists
because of it); the substitution arms initially broke the budget (257 tokens);
`band_table`'s "engine1024" arm was mislabelled (it is the incumbent EADP-256
decode, not the unpruned baseline).
