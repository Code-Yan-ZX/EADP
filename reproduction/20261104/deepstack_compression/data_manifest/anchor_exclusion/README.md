# E0 anchor exclusion: conservative inference supplement

The original `full_manifest.jsonl` and `panel_manifest.jsonl` remain unchanged. The existing E0 fixed 16 questions map to 16 distinct image groups: 1 DEV, 2 CONFIRM and 13 FINAL. E0 identity diagnostics have generated anchor outputs without reading ground truth or scoring; those groups must not be described as never generated in this study. The prior study also exposed these benchmark datasets historically.

`anchor_groups.jsonl` maps all 16 fixed anchor question IDs to their original split and global image group. `excluded_questions.jsonl` lists every CONFIRM/FINAL question in those groups across tasks; the exclusions are 9 DocVQA CONFIRM questions and 85 DocVQA FINAL questions. `inference_questions.jsonl` is the complementary ordered CONFIRM/FINAL question list. The original CONFIRM 128/256 split convention remains unchanged; `confirm_panel_supplement.jsonl` contains a conservative panel with 254 DocVQA, 256 TextVQA, and 256 AI2D groups, while `confirm_panel_excluded.jsonl` preserves its removed rows. No questions from those groups are moved between splits.

`summary.json` binds the original plan, the original full and panel manifests, the build script, and all outputs by SHA256. `verification.json` independently reconstructs the anchor groups and both ordered partitions, checks source and output hashes, verifies no retained group intersects an anchor group, and recounts distinct groups. Neither script reads or produces reference answers, predictions, or scores. The supplemental lists provide a transparent sensitivity analysis; they do not retroactively make historically viewed benchmark data novel.

CONFIRM may be used only after the planned limited candidate and primary references are frozen; FINAL may be evaluated once only after locking a single candidate and two references. Creating these lists does not authorize or perform either generation or scoring. The original lists and all resulting analyses must remain available and separately identified.
